# OnMap.me

[OnMap.me](https://onmap.me) makes one page for a local business in the United States, written so ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude can find and quote it.

- **For business owners:** [how it works](https://onmap.me/how-it-works), [the dashboard](https://onmap.me/panel), [pricing](https://onmap.me/pricing)
- **Free tool:** [How AI sees your site](https://onmap.me/tools/ai-site-check)
- **Guides:** [getting a local business found by AI](https://onmap.me/guides)
- **For developers and agents:** [onmap.me/developers](https://onmap.me/developers) and the [MCP server](https://github.com/onmapme/onmap-mcp) at `https://onmap.me/mcp`
